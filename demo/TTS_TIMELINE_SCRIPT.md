Welcome to EventLedger AI, an intelligent event finance platform combining PayPal payments, account aggregation, and auditable settlement records. Let's create a new event. Instead of manual data entry, we simply describe our gathering in plain English using Gemini AI. Watch as the AI instantly parses our prompt, populating the title, registration fee, attendee targets, and currency automatically. On our events list, we manage two distinct event models: active collections that are currently onboarding attendees, and closed events that are cryptographically locked and settled. Let's inspect our active summit. Here organizers can monitor collection progress, review registered participants, and manage the event lifecycle. Participants join through our public registration portal. Entering their details triggers our payment orchestration engine. For international attendees, our backend calls the PayPal Invoicing API v2, generating a live Sandbox invoice and a dynamic payment QR code ready for checkout. Once the attendee checks out, we verify the transaction against PayPal Sandbox. The backend updates the attendee to verified paid status and logs the transaction reference to our master ledger. With one click, organizers can export a print-ready PDF audit report containing complete transaction breakdowns, attendee verification states, and tamper-proof settlement records. Now let's explore our Financial Hub. Event apps usually suffer from a data blindspot—they only see ticket fees, not real vendor expenses. Governed by the Reserve Bank of India's Account Aggregator framework, financial data is shared strictly through explicit, regulated user consent. Using this consent-driven flow, we select our bank—like APS Bank—and choose between one-time or recurring consent to link our account. Once linked, our external bank balances and categorized expenses appear directly alongside our event collections, organizing multiple financial feeds into a single unified ledger. Our interactive charts break down monthly cashflows and spending categories—such as venue deposits, travel, and logistics—giving organizers a true picture of operational runway. Returning to the home dashboard, our settings panel allows seamless customization, including full dark mode support designed for high-contrast financial monitoring. Finally, let's open our Google Gemini AI Copilot. When greeted with 'hi', it responds naturally. When asked to summarize our financial position, it reads our live telemetry to report exact collections and pending invoices without hallucination. Closing the assistant, our recent activity feed now reflects every live event creation, invoice, and verified payment in real time. EventLedger AI—Intelligent Event Finance and Payments.
